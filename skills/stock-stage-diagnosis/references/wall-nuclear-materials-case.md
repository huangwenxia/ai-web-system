# Reference Case: 沃尔核材 002130 / 09981

Use this as a pattern example, not as permanent market truth. Re-check current data before reusing any price or flow numbers.

## Process Summary

As of 2026-06-04 morning data, 沃尔核材 looked like a theme-driven mark-up followed by large-fund distribution and valuation de-rating, rather than a clean business collapse.

## Stage 1: Build-Up And Acceleration

Approximate period: late 2025 to 2026-01-26.

The stock moved from the high-20s into a final acceleration. From 2026-01-15 to 2026-01-26, A-share price accelerated from around 26-27 to a high near 34.28. Super-large orders were the main force: estimated super-large net inflow was about 16.78 yi CNY (亿元) in the data pull, with main net inflow about 16.32 yi CNY.

Interpretation: AI data-center / high-speed communication cable expectations were being repriced upward. This was not a retail-only push.

## Stage 2: High-Position Exit / Markdown

Approximate period: 2026-01-27 to 2026-03-23.

After the high, large/super-large flow reversed. In the sampled data, main funds net flowed out about 36.06 yi CNY, with super-large outflow about 21.65 yi CNY and large-order outflow about 14.41 yi CNY. Small-order flow was positive by about 38.32 yi CNY.

Interpretation: large money reduced exposure while retail, financing funds, and dip-buying money absorbed supply. The purpose was likely high-position profit-taking and risk reduction after a crowded theme move.

## Stage 3: Box, T-Trading, And Continued Distribution

Approximate period: 2026-03-24 to 2026-06-04.

The stock repeatedly rebounded into resistance and failed. From the sampled data, main funds continued to flow out about 22.19 yi CNY, while small-order flow was positive by about 24.13 yi CNY. Several rallies looked like restarts but ended as upper-shadow or weak-rebound tests.

Interpretation: not a clear re-accumulation. More like inventory management, T-trading, and continued distribution unless large-order flow turns sustained positive and pressure zones are reclaimed.

## Business Diagnosis

The business did not obviously collapse. 2025 annual results were strong: revenue about 84.51 yi CNY, up about 22.00%; attributable net profit about 11.44 yi CNY, up about 34.96%.

The issue was 2026Q1 quality of earnings: revenue grew about 15.52% to about 20.32 yi CNY, but attributable net profit fell about 7.63% to about 2.31 yi CNY; gross margin fell from about 32.53% to about 30.46%. Reported pressure included lower high-margin power/high-voltage and wind-power revenue, raw material price pressure, expense increases, and exchange losses.

The high-speed communication cable story still existed: annual-report language included scaled delivery of 224G high-speed communication cables and 448G samples being delivered for key-customer validation. The market problem was not "the story disappeared"; it was that profit confirmation did not match the earlier valuation premium.

## Real Problem

Main contradiction: narrative premium outran profit verification.

Secondary risks:

- Large/super-large money exited while small money absorbed supply.
- A/H listing and dispersed shareholding made the stock more vulnerable to secondary-market capital rhythm.
- Q1 margin/profit pressure made the market less willing to pay for future expectations.

## Response Pattern

For an empty position: wait for a reclaimed pressure zone plus sustained large-order inflow; do not chase a one-day rebound.

For a light position: observe support retests and whether failed rebounds become reclaimed pressure.

For a heavy position: define a risk line and reduce emotional averaging. A break of support without quick recovery means the stage remains markdown/distribution.

Evidence of improvement should include relative strength versus the index, pressure-zone reclaim, shrinking sell volume, and sustained large/super-large inflow rather than small-order-only support.
